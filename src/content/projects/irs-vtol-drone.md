From September 2024 to June 2025 I was on the National Defense Society's IRS-VTOL drone technical team at Purdue.

- I **configured and installed ArduPilot-based autopilots** on multiple drone platforms and analyzed the communication signals for ground-to-drone connectivity.
- I **contributed to the design and testing** of a VTOL-capable IRS drone, focusing on structural analysis, aerodynamic optimization, and integrating the propulsion, avionics and a live video sensing suite.
