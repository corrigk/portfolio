My sister makes waterproof wax-rope bracelets (Catholic, school-spirit and custom) and hand-painted watercolor keepsakes, so I designed her a shop to sell them.

## What's on the site

- A **shop** filtered by collection, with sizing, charm and color options on each piece.
- **Custom orders**: pick a rope color or charm, or add a name.
- **Our Story**, **FAQ & Shipping**, and a **reviews** section where customers can leave their own.
- A **contact form** and chat, plus a newsletter sign-up for new drops.
