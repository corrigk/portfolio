# How to edit the website (no coding needed)

You can change everything on the site from a web page. Nothing you do can break the whole site: every save is remembered and can be undone.

## Logging in

1. Open the editing page: **https://corrigk.github.io/portfolio/admin/**
2. Click **Login with GitHub** and sign in with your GitHub account (the site owner has already invited you; accept the email invitation first).
3. You'll see a list on the left: **Projects**, **Site Settings** and **Pages**.

## Adding a project

1. Click **Projects**, then **New Project** (top right).
2. Fill in the boxes. The important ones:
   - **Title** and **One-line summary**: what shows on the project card.
   - **Category**: hardware, software or fun.
   - **Cover image**: click it, then *Choose an image* and upload a photo from your computer.
   - **Cover image description**: a short sentence describing the photo (for people using screen readers). Required.
   - **Description**: tell the story of the project. Use the toolbar for bold, lists, headings and pictures.
3. Optional: add more photos under **Gallery**, links (video, GitHub, etc.), and what tools or materials you used.
4. Tick **Featured on home page** only for your 2-3 best projects.
5. Click **Publish** (top), then **Publish now**.

## Changing an existing project

Click **Projects**, click the project, change what you want, then **Publish → Publish now**.

## Editing the About page and your name

- **Pages → About page**: your story and the "Fun stuff" list.
- **Site Settings**: your name, tagline, email, links.

## When does it go live?

About **1-2 minutes** after pressing Publish. Refresh the website to see it. (If you don't see it, wait a minute and refresh again.)

## Photo tips

- Photos straight from a phone are big. If you can, shrink them to about 1600 pixels wide first; it makes the site faster.
- Landscape (wide) photos look best for covers.

## If something looks wrong

- **Can't log in:** make sure you accepted the GitHub invitation email, then try again. If it still fails, message the site owner.
- **Changed something by mistake:** don't worry. Tell the site owner; every earlier version is saved and can be restored in seconds.
- **The site looks broken after a change:** wait 2 minutes and refresh. If it's still wrong, send the owner a screenshot and what you changed.
- **Your edit isn't showing:** you need to press **Publish → Publish now**; saving a draft isn't enough.
