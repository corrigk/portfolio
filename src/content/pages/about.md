I'm Kevin, an Aeronautical and Astronautical Engineering student at Purdue, graduating in May 2027. I grew up an Air Force dependent (born in South Dakota, then Texas, Arizona and Idaho), and I'm driven by a simple goal: keeping pilots like my father in the sky. I'm fascinated by anything that flies, whether it's a soaring airplane or an innovative idea.

My work lives where aerospace meets software. On the hardware side I've built a [hypersonic-flight payload](../projects/rocket-payload/), worked on [Purdue Orbital's airframes](../projects/purdue-orbital/) and an [IRS-VTOL drone](../projects/irs-vtol-drone/). On the software side I build design tools like the [PACTM Wing Explorer](../projects/pactm-wing-explorer/), and I've put that to work in internships at Republic Airways and Micron Technology. See the [timeline](../timeline/) for the whole path.
